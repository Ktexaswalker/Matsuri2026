Program

It will be announced in April

At Matsuri Barcelona, ​​we have a very complete program of activities, with all kinds of incredible artists brought exclusively from Japan, from theater, singing, taiko drumming, shamisen, sumi-e, dance, and martial arts, to super fun children's activities for the whole family.

In the meantime, here's some information about the Bon Odori, the main dance that closes Matsuri every year, and you can participate.

In the Barcelona Ondo, everyone forms a large circle and dances; it's a symbolic element that clearly represents the spirit of this event. If you want to learn and practice it, check out this video!

The complete program will be available next April, so keep an eye on the website and our Instagram!
