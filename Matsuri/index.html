Information about the Event

Matsuri Barcelona is an annual event where you can enjoy Japanese culture, with top artists and performances, numerous exhibitors, Japanese food and drink, a kid's area, and various activities typical of Japanese festivals, so you can enjoy an immersive and fun weekend with your family and friends.
