Matsuri 2026 Poster

We present our famous Matsuri Barcelona poster created every year by the tattoo artist Jun Chihara.

The new 2026 poster reinterprets Japanese tradition with a vibrant energy inspired by ukiyo-e style. The composition combines symbolic creatures, movement and color in a scene full of life which celebrates the festival essence: the connection between Japanese culture, living together and celebrating.

Get official merchandise during the Matsuri or support our crowdfunding campaign and receive exclusive gifts.

Information about the Event

Matsuri Barcelona is an annual event where you can enjoy Japanese culture, with top artists and performances, numerous exhibitors, Japanese food and drink, a kid's area, and various activities typical of Japanese festivals, so you can enjoy an immersive and fun weekend with your family and friends.
