Volunteering

- Open call -

Matsuri is made possible by over 130 volunteers each year. It's an event where many people from diverse backgrounds work together out of a love for intercultural exchange and, especially, to share our passion for Japanese culture.

Anyone over 18 can volunteer at Matsuri after a selection process. It's an event where we work hard to offer the best, but we also always meet wonderful new people, make friends, and have an unforgettable weekend.
