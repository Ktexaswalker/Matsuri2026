Sponsor us

Matsuri Barcelona attracts thousands of visitors each year and is located in a prime Barcelona setting, right on the waterfront in a popular tourist area. The number of attendees has grown steadily, reaching approximately 40,000 in 2024.

By becoming a sponsor of Matsuri, you'll gain advertising exposure and enhance your brand's value through cultural support, international exchange, and community contributions.

It's a fantastic opportunity to promote your brand to thousands of attendees by displaying it at various strategic points throughout the venue and using diverse advertising formats. You'll also have the opportunity to collaborate on sustainable cultural activities at this family-friendly, multigenerational, and culturally vibrant event, which projects a very positive image to the public.
